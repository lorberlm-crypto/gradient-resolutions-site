Parenting plans
Family Fit
$2,135 base package, about 7 hours

Family Fit helps separated and divorcing parents stop arguing about "best interests" and start asking which parent fits this child's temperament in this moment. Each parent charts the child separately from observation, using the Infant Toddler Temperament Charting Tool, version 3 (IT3). We turn those charts into a parenting plan with exchange recipes that work in both homes.

Who it's for: Parents building a parenting plan in a safe, functional home. Where trauma, neglect, or parentification is driving a child's behavior, this work doesn't replace clinical or child-protective services.

The base package (about 7 hours)

SessionFormatTimeWhat happens
1 Joint intake 90 min Each parent is heard without interruption. We check for blockers and confirm the workbook has been received.
2 Private (Parent A) 75 min Intake questions, a DV screen (completed in advance where possible), and temperament charting. Remaining charts are homework with a return date.
3 Private (Parent B) 75 min Same structure as session 2.
4 Joint 3 hours Compare charts, work through the parenting plan, and draft the exchange recipe and the communication channel between homes.

Included

The Family Fit workbook (sent to both parents on the day of intake), the IT3 charting tool, and draft parenting plan language and an exchange recipe.

Add-ons

Two-hour sessions, scheduled when needed. Any issue that must be decided before the plan can be finalized gets its own session. Examples include interim housing while financial disclosure is pending, cohabitation terms, school decisions, and substance use or mental health concerns. Some add-ons may need counsel or a clinical referral. We confirm the path before scheduling, and add-on hours and fees are confirmed in writing before they're booked.

How time works

Hours are estimates. We review the clock at the close of each session. If time runs short, we stop at a natural break and agree on the next step. Four sessions are the start of the process, not the whole process.

Scope: Mediation, not legal advice or clinical treatment.

Parent Coordination
$350 per hour

Some families need extra help making parenting decisions, whether that's part of a court process or after a parenting plan is finalized but isn't working. That's when you might agree to bring in a Parenting Coordinator.

As your Parenting Coordinator, I help parents understand child development, mediate when possible, and make decisions for the parents when necessary, but only within the limits of your court order or signed agreement. I'm not a therapist, and I'm not either parent's attorney. I'm on everyone's side, and most of all on your children's side.

Confidentiality: Parent coordination is not confidential the way mediation is. What you share with me may be shared with the other parent, your attorneys, or the court.

Fees

  • Rate — billed in six-minute increments. Covers sessions, calls, emails, document review, conversations with people like therapists or schools, and writing decisions.$350 / hour
  • Onboarding — one time, when you sign the agreement.$150
  • Prepayment — six hours before we begin. Each parent usually pays half ($1,050).$2,100
  • Testimony — per appearance, including document review. The parent who requests testimony pays before the date.$500

Top-up: When the balance drops below two hours, each parent adds their share before the next session. If it isn't refilled, work pauses unless there's an emergency involving the children's safety. Any remaining balance is refunded when we finish.

For attorneys and mediators
The Pinch Hit

When one issue is holding up an otherwise moving case, the Pinch Hit is a focused, two-hour intervention. Both parties complete two short sections of the Co-Parenting Plan Blueprint workbook before we meet. I review their submissions ahead of time, so I walk in already oriented to the family. Drafting the final agreement stays with the referring professional.

$560 includes workbooks, preparation, and two hours of mediation. A follow-up session for reviewing and signing language is billed separately.
