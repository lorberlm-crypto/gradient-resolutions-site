When a marriage is ending:
A resource for couples navigating divorce
Divorce does not have to be a fight. It can be a process — one where both of you stay in the room while the decisions that will shape your children's lives and your financial future are still being made.

What mediation is — and is not

Mediation isMediation is not
A confidential, voluntary processA legal proceeding or court hearing
Something either party can end at any timeBinding without your signature
Forward-focused — about what works nextTherapy or a determination of whose fault this is
A space where children stay centralSomeone telling you how to parent

What it can cover

A California divorce settlement addresses parenting plans, child support, spousal support, and division of assets and debts. We start with what matters most to you and build from there. If your family has layers — mental health, sobriety, domestic violence, a child with different needs — that is not a reason to avoid this process. It is a reason to find the right mediator.

What it costs

Mediated divorce

Typically $5,000–$15,000 total, and can complete at the six-month minimum California requires.

Litigated divorce

Averages $17,000–$30,000 per person — and can take 18 to 24 months.
